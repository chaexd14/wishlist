# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js 16 App Router, React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS 4, PlopUI, Supabase, and Vercel.

## Users

The primary recipient is the creator's girlfriend, using a private birthday link on her phone, iPad, or computer to choose gifts and describe the exact variants and customizations she wants. The creator uses a private admin area to turn her submitted choices into a real-world purchase checklist.

## Product Purpose

The site makes choosing birthday gifts feel like opening and filling in a personal journal rather than shopping in a public store. Success means the recipient can confidently submit every desired item and detail, and the creator receives a complete, actionable list.

## Positioning

This is a private, affectionate gift-selection experience: checkout confirms a promise from the creator instead of collecting payment.

## Operating Context

The recipient browses a curated catalog, chooses variants, quantities, and optional customization, reviews a cart, then submits it once. The creator maintains gifts and reviews submissions from a protected admin area before purchasing the items offline.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Gifts may define their own option groups, maximum quantity, and customization instructions.
- Checkout contains no payment collection.
- A submitted wishlist preserves the selected gift details as they appeared at submission time.
- The interface must work from 320px phones through iPads and desktop displays.
- Storefront actions must remain touch-friendly, keyboard accessible, and usable with reduced motion.

## Brand Commitments

The experience is warm, personal, romantic, and handmade. The confirmed visual direction is a brown kraft-paper journal with cream sheets, scrapbook layering, and sticker-like controls built from PlopUI. It uses a fixed warm-light presentation rather than dark mode.

## Evidence on Hand

No final product photography, gift catalog, personal letter, girlfriend's name, or real submission data has been supplied. Development examples must remain clearly illustrative and must not invent factual claims.

## Product Principles

- Make every choice feel personal rather than transactional.
- Keep selection clear even when the presentation is playful.
- Preserve every option and customization the recipient submits.
- Let responsive behavior serve the device in her hand.
- Keep the creator's purchasing workflow practical and trustworthy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA, visible keyboard focus, 44px minimum touch targets, readable contrast, semantic form controls, and respect for `prefers-reduced-motion`.
