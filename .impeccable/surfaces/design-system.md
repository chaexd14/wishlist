# Journal design foundation

## Scope and mode

Operate-mode component foundation and internal gallery for every storefront and admin route.

## Confirmed context

The recipient must be able to understand and operate gift selection on phones, iPads, and desktop screens. The creator needs the same primitives in a calmer admin presentation. The fixed world is brown kraft paper, cream journal sheets, scrapbook layers, and PlopUI sticker controls.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** A birthday wishlist behaves like a journal page being filled in, not a miniature online shop. It refuses the generic white ecommerce grid and keeps familiar controls inside a tactile paper world.

**OWN-WORLD:** Kraft is the field, cream paper is the working surface, espresso is the ink, and dusty rose, sage, honey, and terracotta are printed spot colors. Sketchpop lettering and stable sticker tilts provide personality; spacing and standard controls preserve clarity.

**STORY:** The recipient opens something made specifically for her, recognizes each choice immediately, and moves from discovery to a confident submission. The component gallery proves the system can carry actions, options, quantities, notes, feedback, and edge states before product pages are assembled.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** A compact journal masthead sits above one taped cream sheet. The sheet introduces the palette and primary actions without a conventional hero. Below it, primitives move through buttons, tags, fields, choices, quantities, and states in responsive working groups.

**FORM:** seed: none (pinned). The user pinned “brown and a journal style coloring,” “sticker vibe,” PlopUI, kraft paper, warm light only, app wrappers, and responsiveness from PC to iPad to phone.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Memorable move

Important actions behave like freshly pressed stickers while the cream working sheets stay calm and readable. On narrow screens the scrapbook composition tidies itself without losing the material contrast.
